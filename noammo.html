    Shortcuts & controls
    Click
    Select machinery, chop a tree, or place the machine you are holding.
    Shift-click empty ground
    Start box selection; tap the opposite corner to finish. Pressing and releasing Shift, then tapping also works. Hold Shift on a corner to add to your selection.
    Shift-click an object
    Use its quick action: resource collectors collect all contents. Objects without a quick action are selected instead. If another building or group is selected, add the clicked building to your selection without using its quick action. If a tree is selected, select the building without its quick action.
    Shift-hover an upgrader or Grading Bench
    View its last five processed outputs, newest first. The same history is shown when selected. Grading includes the rolled grade; stacks show total and per-item values.
    Box select button
    Tap any two corners, even on occupied tiles. You can also drag from empty ground.
    Move
    Move the selected machine or group, then keep placing copies from inventory. Placement stops when inventory cannot supply another complete copy. Cancel stops repeating.
    Q / E
    Rotate counterclockwise / clockwise. Visible Rotate buttons do the same.
    Escape / right-click
    Cancel placement, movement or box selection; otherwise clear selection. The visible Cancel button works too.
    Edge hover / mouse wheel
    Pan / zoom the factory. Home returns to its center.

    Recipes

    Leek Fiber Extractor

    1 Leek → 2 Leek Fiber

    Each Fiber retains the Leek’s incoming value. Hand-operated; no kinetic connection.

    Crude Gear Bench

    2 Wood → 1 Wooden Gear in 12 seconds · 1 crew

    Hand-operated. Input and output share the lower tile; the gear retains the combined value and weight of its Wood.

    Primitive Conveyor

    10 Wood + 4 Wooden Gears per conveyor.

    Carries up to 2 weight per stack. Stacks must fit as a whole; overweight stacks wait without automatic splitting.

    Machines with internal conveyors require purchased belts as construction components.

    Logger

    1 Wood every 6 seconds · 1 crew

    Place inside one of a tree’s four middle edge tiles, facing outward. Trees are inexhaustible.

    Clay Pit

    1 Clay every 12 seconds · 1 crew

    Manual extraction, without kinetic power. Clay has a base sale value of $0.25.

    Centered output needs two compatible receiving conveyors or a machine covering its full width. If one conveyor feeds the other, the front conveyor receives the cargo; otherwise both must face the same direction.

    Primitive Rock Drill

    2 Chert or 2 Granite every 5 seconds. Select the rock in the machine controls. Connect a Hamster Wheel above or below the middle column.

    Uses the same centered-output rules as Clay Pit. Chert, Granite and Limestone have $0.10 base value; Corundum has $1 base value.

    Copper Drill

    1 Native Copper every 15 seconds. Native Copper has $1.50 base value.

    Connect a Hamster Wheel above or below the middle column. Uses the same centered two-tile output rules as Clay Pit.

    Clay Shaping Bench

    1 Clay → 1 Unfired Brick in 2 seconds, retaining its value.

    2 Clay → 1 Unfired Pot in 4 seconds, worth twice their combined value.

    Hand-operated · 1 crew. Unfired Pots weigh 2; Unfired Bricks weigh 1.

    Pot Spinner

    2 Clay → 1 Unfired Pot in 8 seconds, worth twice their combined value.

    Requires a Hamster Wheel connected above or below its left edge. No operating crew beyond the Wheel.

    Base chances: Poor 20%, Unremarkable 10%, Standard 40%, Fine 25%, Remarkable 5%. Four Brick Seal repairs move Poor's 20 percentage points into Unremarkable. Firing promotes 40% of each tier’s chance, stopping at Remarkable. The Grading Bench makes the roll.

    Clay Kiln

    4 Unfired Bricks → 4 Bricks, or 2 Unfired Pots → 2 Fired Pots.

    Full batches take 8 seconds and 80ku of heat. Each product retains four times its incoming value. Firing promotes 40% of each tier’s chance, stopping at Remarkable. Uses 2 crew.

    Stores up to 250ku of heat. Both inputs accept fuel or unfired products.

    Primitive Furnace

    Charcoal: 4 Wood → 2 Charcoal in 12 seconds, without external heat. The combined incoming cash value is preserved.

    Copper: 4 Native Copper → 4 Molten Copper in 20 seconds, consuming 500ku of heat. Copper retains four times the incoming ore value and promotes its grade chances by one tier. Molding preserves those chances.

    Uses 2 crew and holds up to 1Mu of heat. Select the recipe in the machine controls. Both inputs accept ingredients or fuel; in Charcoal mode, Wood is the ingredient.

    Connect its output directly to an Ingot Molder. Molten metal is not solid conveyor cargo.

    Ingot Molder

    1 Molten Copper → 1 Copper Ingot in 1 second, preserving value. Uses 1 crew and no consumables.

    Input and output share the lower tile of its vertical two-tile footprint. Holds one waiting liquid unit and one finished ingot while molding the next.

    Cast Iron requires a Refractory Caster.

    Leek Infuser

    Adds $0.40 to sellables worth less than $2. Does not consume ingredients during operation.

    Leek Duster

    Multiplies incoming value below $200 by 1.65. Separately promotes 100% of Poor chance to Unremarkable, 50% of Unremarkable to Standard and 25% of Standard to Fine, using the incoming chances simultaneously.

    Its grade effect applies outside the cash limit. Each effect remembers every material treated and cannot repeat on that material, even after processing. Requires a Wheel connected above or below its middle conveyor; transit takes 6 seconds.

    Pebble Pile

    Adds $4 per use, up to 5 uses. Requires a base value of at least $0.50.

    Brick Seal

    Adds $1.25, up to 9 uses. Requires a base value of at least $0.50 and an incoming value below $15.

    Each of four grade-repair uses converts 5 percentage points of Poor chance to Unremarkable on clay products before grading. Repairs carry through shaping and firing; a Pot averages the repairs of its two Clay inputs.

    Primitive Shack

    Multiplies value by 2 once, requiring at least $1.20 base value and incoming value below $2.5k. Two independent three-tile conveyor paths.

    Granite Processor

    Multiplies value by 1.3, up to 6 uses. Requires at least $3.50 base value and incoming value below $500. Two parallel primitive conveyor lanes.

    Copper Chimes

    Multiplies value by 1.25 once. Requires incoming value below $5k.

    Three occupied tiles in a column, with a right-facing primitive conveyor in the middle. Requires kinetic power.

    Primitive Conveyor Bridge

    A three-tile span carrying one stream over an ordinary conveyor. Only its middle tile can overlap a ground conveyor; the entrance and exit need clear space.

    Costs $100 and four unplaced Primitive Conveyors.

    Primitive Stacker

    Combines matching items into stacks of the typed size (1–2). Accepts from three sides and outputs in its facing direction. Requires kinetic power and takes two seconds per outgoing stack.

    Grading Bench

    Grades one stack in 1 second per total weight, using 1 crew.

    Ordinary Clay products start with 20% Poor, 60% Unremarkable and 20% Standard; stone starts at 100% Unremarkable. Native Copper starts at 30% Poor, 40% Unremarkable and 30% Standard. Poor ×0.75, Unremarkable ×1, Standard ×1.5, Fine ×2.5, Remarkable ×8, Exceptional ×40.

    Combining stacks averages actual unit values and pre-grading quality modifiers, keeps each upgrader's highest use count and combines its tags. If any input is graded, the whole stack is graded and cannot roll again. Splitting preserves these properties.

    Leek Planter

    1 Leek every 8 seconds

    Direct edge output, without an internal conveyor.

    Fuel

    1 Wood = 30ku of heat. 1 Charcoal = 240ku of heat.

    Furnaces accept whole fuel items only when their heat fits in the remaining capacity.

    Help

    How values, processing and factory connections work. Open a topic below; individual machine effects and recipes are listed in the Shop and Recipes.

    Material changes renew cash-upgrader uses

    Most manufacturing steps create a new material with fresh cash-upgrader use counts. Leek Duster is an exception: it remembers each material it has treated, even through later transformations. The value you built up is carried into the recipe and scaled by its effect; it does not simply return to base value.

    For example, Primitive Shack can upgrade Native Copper, then upgrade the resulting Copper Ingot again. The new material must still meet the upgrader’s base-value and cash requirements.

    Use limits are shared across all machines of the same kind. Passing through a different Granite Processor does not reset its uses. Moving, stacking or splitting cargo does not renew them either.

    Quality follows separate rules: Brick Seal’s grade repairs carry through shaping and firing, and processing an already graded ingredient does not give the product another grading roll.

    Base value, current value and cash limits

    Base value belongs to the material. Cash upgrades and grading change its current value, without raising that base value. Making a different material changes which base value is checked.

    Upgrader requirements and cash effects apply per item, not to the whole stack. Two items worth $10 each have $20 total value, but each still counts as $10 for eligibility.

    An Upper Limit checks the incoming current value; a successful final upgrade can exceed it. Base Value Required checks base value, while a Lower Bound checks current value. A Use Limit counts successful cash upgrades of that kind.

    Stacking different values and upgrade histories

    A stack behaves as one object. Only matching materials can stack together. Their quantities add, while their current unit values, grade repairs and quality chances are averaged by quantity. Total cash value is preserved.

    For each cash upgrader, the combined stack keeps the highest use count found in its inputs, and retains their other upgrade tags. Uses are not averaged: combining an unused item with one that has exhausted an upgrader leaves the entire stack exhausted for that upgrader.

    Splitting copies the same unit value, use counts and quality information into each portion. If any input is already graded, the whole combined stack becomes graded.

    Grading, repairs and finished value

    The Grading Bench makes one roll for the entire incoming stack and multiplies its current value. Already graded cargo cannot roll again, even after stacking or manufacturing.

    Poor ×0.75 · Unremarkable ×1 · Standard ×1.5 · Fine ×2.5 · Remarkable ×8 · Exceptional ×40.

    Quality treatments change probabilities deterministically; only grading makes a random roll. Metal smelting shifts chances up one tier; clay firing shifts 40% up one tier, stopping at Remarkable. Other form changes preserve chances unless they specify a quality effect.

    Materials and processes can have different starting chances. Select a Grading Bench to see the chances for its cargo. Brick Seal moves Poor chance to Unremarkable on clay products independently of its cash effect, so it can still repair eligible ungraded clay products after its cash upgrades stop.

    Select an upgrader or Grading Bench, or Shift-hover over it, to see its last five outputs. The Grading Bench’s history includes the rolled grade.

    Conveyor capacity and centered outputs

    A Primitive Conveyor holds one cargo stack, up to 2 total weight. Stacking reduces the number of objects, not their total weight. Overweight stacks wait; they are not automatically split to fit a belt.

    A centered two-tile outlet needs both receiving tiles covered by compatible conveyors, or a receiving machine spanning the outlet. A single conveyor is insufficient.

    If one receiving conveyor feeds the other, the front conveyor gets priority. Otherwise both must face the same direction; the centered cargo occupies space across both belts. Opposing or mismatched directions block the outlet.

    Kinetic power and direct connections

    A Basic Hamster Wheel has two outputs, one at the center of each long edge. A kinetic intake must touch an output and face it. Nearby machines are not powered merely by being close to a Wheel.

    A connected Conveyor Drive powers primitive conveyors within its highlighted area. Drills, Pebble Sifters, Pot Spinners and Leek Dusters need a direct Wheel connection instead. Either intake on a kinetic drill, Pot Spinner or Leek Duster is sufficient; it does not need two Wheels.

    Power loss pauses work. It does not build up a backlog of completed production cycles.

    Furnace batches, fuel and liquid metal

    Furnaces wait for a full recipe batch and enough stored heat, then spend the batch’s heat when work starts. Either ingredient input can accept fuel or ingredients. A whole fuel item must fit within the remaining heat capacity.

    A finished batch can leave in smaller portions as the receiving belt clears. Seeing fewer than a full batch waiting at the outlet does not mean the furnace made less.

    Molten metal does not travel on solid conveyors. Connect a Primitive Furnace’s outlet directly to an Ingot Molder’s intake.

    Collectors, inventory and dismantling

    Collectors hold factory cargo until collected. Collecting transfers material quantities into inventory; upgraded cash values, grades and upgrade histories are not retained there. Sell upgraded cargo through a Sell Tube to receive its current value.

    Pick up a machine before dismantling it in Inventory. Dismantling returns its construction materials and component machines, with no cash refund. The two starter Planters, one Basket and one Sell Tube are protected when you have no extras.

    Selection, quick actions and controls

    Shift-click empty ground to start box selection, then tap the opposite corner. The Box select button can start on occupied ground too.

    With no other building selected, Shift-click a collector to collect everything. With a building or group selected, Shift-click another building to add it to that selection instead.

    Q/E or the Rotate buttons rotate placement and moves. Escape, right-click or Cancel ends the current action. After moving, placement continues with copies from inventory until another complete copy is unavailable or you cancel.

    Hover near the factory edges to pan, use the mouse wheel or zoom buttons to zoom, and click Home to recenter. The Factory’s “Shortcuts & controls” guide lists the controls while you build.
