Getting started
  1. Place your free Planter and Basket. Point the Planter’s edge output into the Basket.
  2. Collect Leeks. Sell 10 for $1 and keep 10 to buy a Fiber Extractor.
  3. Put the Extractor between the Planter and Basket, or assign a Basket hauler.
  4. Click a tree: five chops harvest 1–5 Wood. At most five chops per second.
  5. Save for a Wheel, Drive and Primitive Upgraders. Connect the Drive’s intake to a long Wheel edge.
